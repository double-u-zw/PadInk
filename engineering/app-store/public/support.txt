需要搭载 Apple Silicon（M 系列）且运行 macOS 14+、iPadOS 17+、可传数据 USB 线；手写需要兼容 Apple Pencil。当前界面为简体中文。PadInk 镜像自己的 Mac 主屏并发送笔／触控输入，文稿由目标应用保存，第三方应用另行安装。
USB：两端运行，iPad 解锁且保持前台，系统询问时信任电脑，再在两端允许 PadInk 配对。VPN 保持开启。
有连接无画面：检查 Mac 屏幕录制权限，按系统提示退出重开。
有画面无输入：检查 Mac 辅助功能、目标文稿焦点与笔工具；手指操作需 iPad 设置开启。
不连接：检查数据线、系统信任、设备解锁、两端版本与前台状态；先重连，不卸载、不删除配对。
缩放不可用：能力依赖目标应用与文稿焦点；用应用自己的控件。PowerPoint 缩放可能需要自动化权限。

Requires an Apple Silicon (M-series) Mac with macOS 14+, iPadOS 17+ and a USB data cable; handwriting needs a compatible Apple Pencil. The interface is Simplified Chinese. The active Mac application saves your documents and must be installed separately.
Keep both apps open and iPad unlocked/in the foreground. Trust the Mac if iPadOS asks, then allow PadInk pairing on both devices. Keep the VPN enabled. A connection without video indicates Screen Recording permission. Video without input indicates Accessibility permission, document focus or the pen tool. Finger control must be enabled in iPad settings. Check cable, system trust, versions and foreground state for connection problems; reconnect before changing pairing. Zoom depends on the target application; PowerPoint zoom may request Automation permission.
